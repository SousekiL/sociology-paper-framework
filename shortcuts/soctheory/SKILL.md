# Soctheory

Build theory rather than a methods section. Start with the sociological puzzle: what varies, for whom, relative to what comparison, and why the pattern is not obvious.

Use broad model knowledge to select 2–4 genuinely competing theoretical lenses. For each, specify its mechanism, observable implication, scope condition, and what finding would weaken it. Distinguish a theoretical construct from its empirical proxy. End with one integrative argument and 2–3 testable propositions; do not invent citations when the user has not requested a verified literature search.
