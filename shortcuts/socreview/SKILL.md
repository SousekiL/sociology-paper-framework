# Socreview

Provide a literature-oriented research map, not a fabricated bibliography. Begin with the topic's core concepts, population, setting, and likely disciplinary debates.

Use the model's academic knowledge to organize 3–5 research streams, their central questions, typical evidence, disagreements, and unresolved tensions. If a private local literature library exists, use verified local records as optional leads; otherwise provide targeted Chinese and English search strings plus journals/datasets to inspect. Clearly label unverified leads and do not invent authors, article titles, DOIs, or empirical results. End with a feasible research gap and the evidence needed to substantiate it.
