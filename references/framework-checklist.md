# Sociology Journal Research-Framework Checklist

## From a topic to a question

Rewrite a broad topic as: “Under what conditions does what mechanism make X affect Y; for whom, through whom, and why does it differ?” Specify the population, time and place, outcome, and comparison baseline. The question must be falsifiable with evidence.

## Match methods to claims

| Goal | Suitable design | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe a distribution or difference | Probability samples, administrative statistics, reproducible text statistics | Description or association |
| Explain a mechanism | Process tracing, in-depth interviews, ethnography, mixed methods | Conditional mechanism account |
| Estimate a policy or event effect | Panel fixed effects, DiD, RDD, matching, event study | Causal claim only with identification assumptions and diagnostics |
| Compare institutional logics | Multi-case comparison, QCA, comparative-historical analysis | Bounded typology or mechanism proposition |

## Common China data entry points

- CGSS: China General Social Survey; stratification, attitudes, family, work, and participation.
- CFPS: China Family Panel Studies; individual–family–community panel data for life-course and inequality research.
- CLDS: China Labor-force Dynamics Survey; employment, labor relations, mobility, and social insurance.
- CHARLS: China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study; later-life health, care, family, and retirement.
- CHFS: China Household Finance Survey; wealth, debt, finance, and household decisions.
- CHNS: China Health and Nutrition Survey; long-run changes in health, consumption, and community conditions.
- China City and provincial statistical yearbooks, censuses, and National Bureau of Statistics data for contextual and policy-intensity measures.
- China Judgements Online, government open data, and public recruitment/platform data; document collection rules, missingness, compliance, and reproducibility.

State whether data are public, require an institutional application, or contain sensitive geographic or individual information. Do not imply access to microdata that you do not possess.
